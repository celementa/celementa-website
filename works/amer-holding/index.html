Amer Holding

A diversified business group in Abu Dhabi, operating across many industries.

Duration4 weeks
IndustryHolding
ClientAmer Holding
Start a project

AMER Holding’s website builds a strong corporate identity that aligns with its mission of “shaping sustainable progress across sectors.” The site uses a clean, professional design to highlight its long-term legacy (“50+ Years of Legacy”) and its broad industrial footprint.

Through a well-structured “Our Industries” section, it clearly communicates its diversified portfolio — from real estate development to contracting, automotive services, and healthcare.

The “Strategic Projects & Flagship Entities” module gives concrete proof of AMER’s impact, referencing projects like Radiant Square and AK Tower.

Overall, the site reflects AMER Holding’s brand as a stable, mature, and visionary conglomerate.
