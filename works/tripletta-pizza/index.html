Tripletta Pizza

A neighborhood pizza brand that blends artisanal craftsmanship with a friendly, local vibe.

Duration5 weeks
IndustryRestaurant
ClientTripletta Pizza
Start a project

Tripletta Pizza’s website captures the warmth and character of a local, artisanal pizzeria. The design is inviting, with friendly copywriting (“La pizza de quartier qui a du caractère”) and rich visuals of pizza-making, ingredients, and the restaurant ambiance.

The site structure emphasizes key customer touchpoints: menu, ordering, locations, and the story behind the brand. It’s not just about the food — Tripletta communicates its identity as a neighborhood staple, combining craft, quality, and community.

The visual style (photos of dough, ovens, toppings) reinforces the hand-made, premium feel, positioning Tripletta as more than fast food: it’s a culinary craftsmanship experience.
