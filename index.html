Do you work on performance-driven projects?

Yes. Every project at Celementa starts with clear KPIs, traffic, leads, or conversions. We focus on measurable outcomes, not vanity metrics.

Can you help us with marketing and automation?

Absolutely. We build complete systems that connect your website, CRM, and campaigns, using tools like HubSpot, Make, and Zapier to automate lead flows and improve ROI.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on the scope: • Landing page or mini-site: 2–3 weeks • Corporate website: 4–6 weeks • SEO & automation setup: 1–2 weeks We always define clear milestones and deliver on schedule.

Can you improve an existing website?

Definitely. We can audit, redesign, or rebuild your existing platform to improve performance, SEO, and conversions, without starting from scratch.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. We collaborate with companies across Europe, the Middle East, and North America. Our global setup allows us to manage projects across time zones efficiently.

What kind of companies do you usually work with?

We partner with SMEs, startups, and corporate groups in sectors like real estate, hospitality, finance, tech, and B2B services, all seeking digital growth and measurable impact.

How much does a project cost?

Our pricing depends on the scope, complexity, and deadlines. Every quote is custom-built and transparent, no hidden fees, no surprises. We also offer cost-effective plans for small and mid-sized projects.

Do you offer ongoing support after launch?

Yes. We provide maintenance, SEO monitoring, analytics reports, and campaign optimization, ensuring long-term performance, not just delivery.

How do I get started?

Simply book a free discovery call. We’ll discuss your goals, audit your current setup, and create a tailored action plan, no obligation.
